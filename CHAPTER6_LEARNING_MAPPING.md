## 3. Bilingual Terminology Reference

| English Term | Nepali Term | Spoken Pronunciation | Pedagogical Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ten** | **दश** | Dasha | The cardinal quantity 10 |
| **Numeral 10** | **सङ्ख्या १०** | Sankhya Dasha | Mathematical representation of ten |
| **Counting** | **गन्ती / गणना** | Ganti / Ganana | Sequential enumeration 1 to 10 |
| **Make Ten** | **१० बनाउने** | Dasha Banaune | Combining two sub-groups to total 10 |
| **Basket** | **टोकरी** | Tokari | Container holding counted items |
| **Garden** | **बगैँचा** | Bagaincha | Natural scene with countable flowers |
| **Writing** | **लेखन** | Lekhana | Motor formation of numeral glyphs |
| **Chalkboard** | **पाटी / स्लेट** | Paati / Slate | Traditional slate for writing practice |
| **How many?** | **कति वटा?** | Kati wata? | Inquiring quantity |
| **Match** | **जोडा मिलाउनुहोस्** | Joda milaunuhos | Connecting quantity to numeral |

## 4. Assessment & Mastery Indicators
- Child counts 10 items in a group accurately without repeating or skipping.
- Child immediately identifies `१०` and `10` when surrounded by other single-digit numbers.
- Child understands that dividing 10 into $4$ and $6$ still totals 10.
- Child correctly draws `10` and `१०` following standard stroke direction without inversions.
