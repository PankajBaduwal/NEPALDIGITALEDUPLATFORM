## Learning Objectives and Curriculum Mapping

**Textbook:** मेरो गणित, कक्षा १ (२०७६)  
**Curriculum Authority:** Curriculum Development Centre (CDC), Sanothimi, Bhaktapur, Nepal

## 1. CDC Learning Outcomes & Simulation Mapping

| Textbook Learning Outcome (CDC) | नेपाली सक्षमता विवरण | Addressed in Simulation | Specific Simulation Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Count objects from 11 to 20 without repetition or omission | ११ देखि २० सम्मका वस्तुहरू गन्ती गर्न | **Simulation 01 (Number Garden)** | 3D countable apples, flowers, balloons, butterflies with click-lock counter and celebration |
| Understand that 11–19 consist of 1 ten and additional ones | १० मा थप वस्तु जोडी ११ देखि १९ सम्मका सङ्ख्या बनाउन | **Simulation 02 (Make a Group of Ten)** | Visual 10-bundle tied with golden ribbon + loose unit cubes, displaying $10 + N = \text{Total}$ |
| Recognize 20 as two groups of ten | २० लाई दशका दुई पूरा समूहका रूपमा बुझ्न | **Simulation 02 & 03** | 2 ten-bundles ($10 + 10 = 20$) and 2 ten-rods with 0 ones |
| Represent place value (Tens and Ones) | स्थानमान तालिकामा दहाइ र एकाइ देखाउन | **Simulation 03 (Tens & Ones Explorer)** | Two-column place value board (दहाइ / एकाइ) with 10-unit rods and unit cubes |
| Locate numbers on a number line, identify before, after, between | सङ्ख्यारेखामा सङ्ख्याहरू पहिचान गरी अघिल्लो, पछिल्लो र बीचको सङ्ख्या भन्न | **Simulation 04 (Number Line Adventure)** | 0–20 stepping stones river path, before/after/between mission modes |
| Pronounce and write number names from 11 to 20 in Devanagari | ११ देखि २० सम्मका सङ्ख्याका नाम (एघार देखि बीस) चिन्न | **Simulation 05 (Challenge World)** | Number Name Shop matching numerals to word cards with native audio speech |
| Compare numbers (smallest and largest) | ११ देखि २० सम्मका सङ्ख्याहरूको तुलना गरी सानो र ठूलो छुट्याउन | **Simulation 05 (Challenge World)** | Comparison Mountain with 4-number sets to pick minimum and maximum |
| Order numbers in ascending and descending sequences | सङ्ख्याहरूलाई बढ्दो (सानोबाट ठूलो) र घट्दो (ठूलोबाट सानो) क्रममा राख्न | **Simulation 05 (Challenge World)** | Train car ordering puzzle with draggable/clickable number slots |
