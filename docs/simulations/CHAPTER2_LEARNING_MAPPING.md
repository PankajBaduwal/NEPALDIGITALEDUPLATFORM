## Direct Competency & Activity Mapping

| Textbook Activity / Page | CDC Learning Outcome | Simulation Implementation | Pedagogical Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **चित्र हेरेर सीधा र बाङ्गो छुट्याउने** (Page 12-14) | Observe pictures in nature/village and recognize straight vs. curved lines. | **Simulation 01:** `StraightCurvedLineExplorer` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH2-01`) | 3D village scene containing straight road, curved road, straight railway, winding river, straight bridge, fence, garden boundary, straight stick, and hanging rope. |
| **तानिएको डोरी र खुकुलो डोरी** (Page 15) | Understand that a taut, stretched rope is straight; a loose rope bends into a curve. | **Simulation 01 Level 3:** Dynamic Rope Physics | Interactive slider allowing students to stretch a loose curved rope into a straight taut rope and see dynamic state updates. |
| **सीधा रेखामा निलो र बाङ्गो रेखामा रातो रङ भरौँ** (Page 16-17) | Color straight lines blue and curved lines red. | **Simulation 02:** `LineColoringSimulation` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH2-02`) | Interactive Blue crayon (सीधा रेखा) and Red crayon (बाङ्गो रेखा) with isolated lines and textbook pictures (Ice-cream, Boat, House). |
| **थोप्लाहरू जोडेर रेखा बनाउने** (Page 18-19) | Join dots to draw straight lines, zigzag lines, and curved lines. | **Simulation 03:** `ConnectTheDotsSimulation` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH2-03`) | Pointer & touch-based dot grid with magnetic snapping, pentatonic musical chimes, collinear alignment, and dot-to-dot kite drawing. |
| **सिधा किनारा भएको वस्तु प्रयोग गरी रेखा खिच्ने** (Page 20) | Draw straight lines by guiding a pencil along the straight edges of rulers, books, and boxes. | **आफैँ रेखा कोर्ने र चित्र बनाउने** (Page 21) | Freely trace and draw straight and curved lines on paper and slate. Combine lines to draw familiar objects (sun rays, ladder). | **Simulation 05:** `LineDrawingPlayground` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH2-05`) | Interactive chalkboard/slate with tracing templates (horizontal, vertical, slanted, wave, loops), color picker, and line-based stickers (Sun, Ladder). |

## Assessment Competencies Verified
1. **Identification:** Identifying whether a line is straight (सीधा) or curved (बाङ्गो) with 100% precision.
2. **Color Categorization:** Strict adherence to textbook standard (Blue for straight, Red for curved).
3. **Tool Utilization:** Knowing which physical objects possess straight edges and using them to create straight lines.
4. **Drawing Fluency:** Tracing and producing straight, zigzag, and curved paths accurately.
