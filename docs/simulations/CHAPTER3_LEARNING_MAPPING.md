## Detailed Activity & Competency Mapping

| Textbook Page & Activity | CDC Competency & Learning Outcome | Simulation Implementation | Pedagogical Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **आकारहरूको पहिचान** (Pages 22–24) | Recognize the three primary shapes: वृत्त (Circle), त्रिभुज (Triangle), and चतुर्भुज (Quadrilateral). | **Simulation 01:** `ShapeExplorer3D` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH3-01`) Level 1 & 2 | Genuine 3D procedural models of pure geometric shapes and real-world objects. Children can rotate the objects 360° to understand that a circle is round, a triangle has 3 sides and 3 corners, and a quadrilateral has 4 sides and 4 corners. |
| **वस्तु र आकारको जोडा मिलाउने** (Pages 25–26) | Match everyday physical objects with their 2D geometric shape representations. | **Simulation 02:** `ShapeMatchingSimulation` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH3-02`) | Interactive drag & drop into 3 bins: Circle (थाली, घडी, मादल, पाङ्ग्रा), Triangle (ट्राफिक सङ्केत, छाना, ध्वजा, ह्याङ्गर), and Quadrilateral (किताब, ढोका, झ्याल, उपहारको बट्टा). |
| **आकारहरूमा रङ भर्ने** (Pages 27–28) | Colour shapes according to official textbook colour guidelines: **Triangle = Red**, **Quadrilateral/Rectangle = Blue**, **Circle = Green**. | **Simulation 03:** `ShapeColoringSimulation` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH3-03`) | Interactive colour palette with instant visual and audio verification. Children colour isolated shapes and composite scenes (cottage & sun, school bus, robot) adhering strictly to CDC colours. |
| **सबैभन्दा सानो वा ठूलो आकार छान्ने** (Page 29) | Compare geometric shapes of identical categories by relative size to identify the smallest or largest in a group. | **Simulation 01:** `ShapeExplorer3D` Level 4 (Shape Comparison) | Displays multiple circles, triangles, and quadrilaterals of varying dimensions; prompts students to find the smallest circle, largest triangle, or smallest quadrilateral. |
| **आफ्नो वरिपरिको वातावरणमा आकार खोज्ने** (Pages 30–31) | Discover geometric shapes embedded in school, home, and outdoor environments. | **Simulation 04:** `ShapeDetectiveSimulation` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH3-04`) | Interactive 3-location scavenger hunt: Classroom (कक्षाकोठा), Playground (खेलमैदान), and Nepalese Village (नेपाली गाउँ). Spotting shapes triggers highlight overlays and educational audio commentary. |
| **आकारहरू जोडेर चित्र बनाउने** (Pages 32–33) | Synthesize basic geometric shapes to construct composite drawings and models. | **Simulation 05:** `ShapeBuilderSimulation` (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH3-05`) | Construction board allowing children to drag, rotate, scale, and color circles, triangles, squares, and rectangles to build houses, robots, buses, flowers, and vehicles. |

## Terminology Alignment
- **वृत्त (Circle):** गोलो आकार, कुनै कुना नभएको।
- **त्रिभुज (Triangle):** तीनवटा सिधा किनारा र तीनवटा कुना भएको आकार।
- **चतुर्भुज (Quadrilateral):** चारवटा सिधा किनारा र चारवटा कुना भएको आकार (वर्ग र आयत दुवै यसका उदाहरण हुन्)।
