# Class 1 Mathematics Chapter 4: Learning Objectives & Curriculum Mapping

## 1. Official Textbook Source
- **Textbook:** मेरो गणित, कक्षा १ (२०७६) – Curriculum Development Centre (CDC), Sanothimi, Bhaktapur, Nepal.
- **Chapter:** Chapter 4 – ९ सम्मका सङ्ख्याहरू (Numbers up to 9)
- **Pedagogical Framework:** Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract (CPA Approach).

---

## 2. Topic Breakdown & Simulation Alignment

| Curriculum Section | Textbook Topic & Page Range | Target Learning Outcome | Assigned Simulation & Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Section 1: धेरै र थोरै (Many and Few)** | मेरो गणित, पृ. ३०–३४ | Compare two sets of concrete objects; identify which group has "धेरै" (more) and which has "थोरै" (fewer/less) using visual correspondence. | **Simulation 01: धेरै र थोरै चिनौँ (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH4-01`)**<br>- 3D baskets containing random quantities 1 to 9.<br>- Side-by-side comparative inspection.<br>- 5 difficulty tiers (obvious contrast to 1-item difference).<br>- 15-question interactive evaluation quiz. |
| **Section 2: १ देखि ९ सम्म गणना (Counting 1 to 9)** | मेरो गणित, पृ. ३५–४२ | Count objects in sets of 1 to 9 sequentially using one-to-one touch matching without skipping or double-counting. | **Simulation 02: १ देखि ९ सम्म गनौँ (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH4-02`)**<br>- 9 themed garden stages with familiar items (1 mushroom, 2 butterflies, 3 rabbits, 4 apples, 5 balloons, 6 mangoes, 7 fish, 8 birds, 9 flowers).<br>- Touch confirmation lock preventing accidental multi-clicks.<br>- Spoken pronunciation in Nepali and English. |
| **Section 3: वस्तु र सङ्ख्या सम्बन्ध (Number Association)** | मेरो गणित, पृ. ४३–४८ | Connect quantities of objects to their abstract numeral representations in both Devanagari (१–९) and English (1–9), and their verbal names (एक–नौ / One–Nine). | **Simulation 03: वस्तु र सङ्ख्या मिलाऔँ (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH4-03`)**<br>- Dual-column matching board.<br>- Progressive rounds (Round 1: 1–3, Round 2: 4–6, Round 3: 7–9, Round 4: Mixed 1–9).<br>- Instant visual and acoustic feedback with stars. |
| **Section 4: सङ्ख्या लेखन (Number Writing & Tracing)** | मेरो गणित, पृ. ४९–५६ | Learn the correct handwriting stroke order, direction, and shape formation for numerals १–९ and 1–9. | **Simulation 04: सङ्ख्या लेख्न सिकौँ (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH4-04`)**<br>- Digital wooden chalkboard slate.<br>- Vector guide paths with starting dots and directional arrows.<br>- "लेख्ने तरिका हेर्नुहोस्" animated demonstration.<br>- Touch/mouse chalk canvas with forgiving accuracy evaluation for 5–7-year-olds. |
| **Section 5: सङ्ख्याको क्रम (Number Sequence & Order)** | मेरो गणित, पृ. ५७–६२ | Understand the ascending order of numbers from 1 to 9; identify missing numbers in a sequence; recognize immediate before/after numbers. | **Simulation 05: सङ्ख्या क्रम मिलाऔँ (`SIM-MATH-G1-CH4-05`)**<br>- Stepping stones river crossing game.<br>- Card sequencing train (ascending 1 to 9).<br>- Missing number chains (`1, 2, ?, 4, 5`).<br>- Before & After number recognition challenges. |

## 4. Assessment & Mastery Criteria
- **Classroom Observables:**
  - Student can correctly identify the larger/smaller pile without counting when difference is large, and by counting when difference is subtle.
  - Student touches each object exactly once during counting.
  - Student writes numerals starting from top stroke downwards.
  - Student identifies that `४` follows `३` and precedes `५`.
- **System Telemetry:**
  - Completion status (`COMPLETED` or `IN_PROGRESS`) and score (0–100) are persisted in MongoDB `simulation_activity` records for teacher review.
