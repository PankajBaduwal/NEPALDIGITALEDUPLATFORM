## 2. Class Teacher Dashboard Features & Access

When any of the assigned teachers logs in to `/teacher`:
1. **Section Supervision Banner:** The dashboard automatically identifies that the teacher is the designated Class Teacher for their respective section.
2. **Attendance Management:** Class Teachers take daily morning roll-call across their section, recording Present, Late, Leave, or Absent status for all 9 students.
3. **Student Profile & Roster:** View full student demographics, admission numbers, roll numbers, guardian contact numbers, and device accessibility status.
4. **Examination Verification & Approval:** Verify and endorse terminal examination marks entered by subject teachers before formal publication.
5. **Report Card Generation:** Instant generation of CDC 2078 terminal grade sheets with letter grades, GPAs, and teacher remarks.
