## 2. Subject Allocation Workflow & Verification

Each teacher has been allocated specific subjects and sections according to their academic qualification and pedagogical experience.

When a teacher logs in (e.g. `dhambojhi_t_01` for Physics/Science or `dhambojhi_t_03` for Mathematics):
1. **Teaching Classes Tab:** Displays all allocated classes with grade, section, student strength, and weekly periods.
2. **Subject Workspace:** Teachers can enter the workspace of any assigned subject to view:
   - Direct CDC Textbook PDF streaming link.
   - Chapter Table of Contents with start and end pages.
   - Smart Board lesson launcher.
   - Quiz Assessment studio.
   - Assignment studio with student submission tracker.
   - Terminal examination marks entry sheet.
