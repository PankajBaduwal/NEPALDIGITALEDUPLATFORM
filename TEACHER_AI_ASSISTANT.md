# Teacher AI Teaching Assistant

Teacher Dashboard → Insights → AI Teaching Assistant, and the AI tab in a subject workspace.
- Classes/subjects come from the teacher's allocations. Selecting an unassigned class or subject returns 403. "Search other classes" widens to all classes but only for the teacher's own subjects.
- Tasks: ask, explain, summarise chapter, teaching notes, worked examples, practice questions, lesson outline, revision sheet, classroom activities, explain diagram/formula. Curriculum frameworks and teacher guides (staff-only documents) are searchable by teachers only.
- Nothing is published from this screen. Quizzes and assignments use the existing Studios (see AI_QUIZ_GENERATION.md, AI_ASSIGNMENT_GENERATION.md).
