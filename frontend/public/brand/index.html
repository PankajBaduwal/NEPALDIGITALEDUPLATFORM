The idea

Two pages, one connection, one nation.

The symbol is an N for Nepal, built the way the platform is built.

  • Two pages. The two green stems are the pages of an open book: knowledge, teachers and students on either side.
  • One connection. The navy diagonal is the single link that joins them: one platform connecting every school and college.
  • A pennant. The small crimson triangle on the right page echoes Nepal's pennant flag and points forward: progress. The open space beside the diagonal repeats the flag's triangle.
  • Simple by construction. Three flat shapes and one accent, no gradients, shadows or 3D. It reads at 16 px and holds up in one colour.
Construction on a 128 unit grid. Gaps between shapes are a constant 7 units, true transparency, so the mark works on any background.

Typography

Manrope

A clean geometric sans-serif with an institutional tone, open-licence (SIL OFL) and already used by the platform.

  • Line 1, “NEPAL DIGITAL”: Manrope ExtraBold 800, slight positive tracking.
  • Line 2, “EDUCATION PLATFORM”: Manrope SemiBold 600, wide tracking (0.165 em), tracked to match line 1 exactly.
  • Name: always written in full, “NEPAL DIGITAL EDUCATION PLATFORM”.
  • Nepali text: pair with Noto Sans Devanagari.

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the height of the crimson pennant on every side (about one fifth of the symbol's height).

Minimum size

  • Horizontal logo: 160 px wide (40 mm in print).
  • Stacked logo: 110 px wide (28 mm).
  • Icon: 16 px, using icon-small.svg below 33 px.

Do not

  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or add shadows or gradients.
  • Rearrange the symbol's shapes or change the name's wording.
  • Place the colour logo on busy images; use a mono or dark version.
