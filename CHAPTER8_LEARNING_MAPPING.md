## 3. Bilingual Terminology Reference

| English Term | Nepali Term | Spoken Pronunciation | Pedagogical Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subtraction** | **घटाउ** | Ghatau | Operation of taking away items from a set |
| **Subtract / Take away** | **घटाउनुहोस् / हटाउनुहोस्** | Ghataunuhos / Hataunuhos | Removing objects from a group |
| **Minus Sign (−)** | **घटाउ चिन्ह (−)** | Ghatau Chinha | Operator symbol for subtraction |
| **Equals Sign (=)** | **बराबर चिन्ह (=)** | Barabar Chinha | Equivalence operator |
| **Remainder / Left** | **बाँकी** | Baanki | Objects left after removal |
| **Difference** | **फरक** | Pharaka | Numerical variance between two sets |
| **More** | **बढी** | Badhi | Larger quantity in comparison |
| **Fewer / Less** | **कम** | Kama | Smaller quantity in comparison |
| **Subtract Zero** | **शून्यको घटाउ** | Shunyako Ghatau | $A - 0 = A$; quantity remains unchanged |
| **Vertical Subtraction** | **ठाडो घटाउ** | Thado Ghatau | Column-based subtraction layout |

## 4. Assessment & Mastery Indicators
- Child physically takes away $M$ items from $N$ items and accurately counts the remainder $N - M$.
- Child recognizes that removing 0 items leaves the full count intact ($A - 0 = A$).
- Child computes vertical subtraction columns accurately without confusion.
- Child can pair objects 1-to-1 between two groups to isolate the numerical difference.
- Child identifies missing numbers in subtraction equations ($7 - [ ] = 3 \implies 4$).
